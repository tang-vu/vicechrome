# 45–60 second demo

1. **0–5s:** Show the opening coupe and “ENTER THE GARAGE.”
2. **5–11s:** Choose Sunset Courier, name the build, and open “DESIGN YOUR PANEL ART.”
3. **11–27s:** In the genuine Unlayer editor, use Draw to make a conspicuous diagonal stroke over the commission artwork (or add your name with Text). Click Unlayer Save.
4. **27–37s:** Hold the garage view long enough to see the same mark on the door. Tap before/after once.
5. **37–47s:** Press “ROLL OUT” and show the shutter, light sweep, and boulevard shot.
6. **47–60s:** Click “SAVE COVER,” open the downloaded PNG, and point to the same visible mark. Mention the separate flat-art download only if time allows.

Keep the cursor out of the vehicle shot. Use original sample art; do not include personal uploads in public evidence.
